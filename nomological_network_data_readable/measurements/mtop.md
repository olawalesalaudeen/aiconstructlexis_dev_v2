## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers here, MTOP is most commonly treated as a benchmark dataset for multilingual task-oriented semantic parsing, with definitions centering on intent and slot filling. It is also described as containing compositional representations for complex nested queries across multiple languages and domains, and one description says it covers 6 languages and 11 domains.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use MTOP to measure one behavioral indicator, Semantic parsing, and no constructs are linked to it. The linked claims converge on semantic parsing: papers describe MTOP as a semantic parsing benchmark and as a benchmark for multilingual task-oriented semantic parsing, with attention to intent and slot filling.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked to MTOP, but papers do use it alongside other semantic parsing benchmarks. One paper presents it with SM-CalFlow and GeoQuery as part of "three popular diverse semantic parsing benchmarks," and another evaluates across four semantic parsing tasks that span "intent and slot filling (MTop)."
