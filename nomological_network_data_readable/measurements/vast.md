**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, VAST is most commonly described as a stance detection dataset or benchmark built around varied targets. Definitions say each instance contains a sentence, a target, and a stance label, with labels given as Pro, Con, or Neutral; one paper adds that it covers a wide range of targets and is used to assess generalization in zero-shot and few-shot settings, while another frames it as an inherently zero-shot stance detection dataset and notes use of the original publisher splits.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use VAST to measure stance detection. In the recorded links, it is connected to one behavioral indicator, stance detection, and not linked to any constructs or other measurement instruments, so its reported use is concentrated on that single behavioral indicator rather than spread across multiple constructs.

**Space of uses.** Across the cited studies, VAST appears as part of experimental evaluations of proposed stance detection methods. Papers report using it alongside SEM16, PStance, and SemEval-2016 to evaluate methods, and one paper describes results on it as part of validations covering zero-shot, few-shot, and challenging scenarios.
