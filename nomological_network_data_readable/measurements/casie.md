# CASIE
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed papers, the prevailing usage is as a cybersecurity event extraction or event detection dataset. Definitions describe it as focused on cybersecurity incidents with trigger and argument annotations, and some descriptions specify five event types, Databreach, Phishing, Ransom, Discover, and Patch; one paper also describes it as identifying security-related incidents from technical reports and news, while another places its evaluation setting in cyber threat reports.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link CASIE to one construct and one behavioral indicator. It is used to measure Event argument extraction, and one paper also uses it in an evaluation framed as measuring Generalization, describing results from "extensive evaluations across six datasets" to demonstrate generalizability. In the surveyed work, these measurement claims center on those two targets rather than a wider spread of constructs or indicators.

**Space of uses.** No other measurement instrument is linked to CASIE. Across the cited uses, papers place it within broader experimental suites, for example as one of "16 IE benchmarks" or within evaluations spanning six datasets, and one table describes datasets including CASIE by whether they are used for training, evaluation, or both.
