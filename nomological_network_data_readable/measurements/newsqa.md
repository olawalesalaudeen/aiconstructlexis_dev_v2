# NewsQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 6 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across definitions, NewsQA is most commonly described as a news-based question answering or machine comprehension dataset. Papers characterize it as requiring answers from long passages, with some emphasizing comprehension and extraction from long, realistic passages, while another specifies CNN articles paired with questions and answers; one study also refers to the MRQA version of NewsQA.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the papers, NewsQA is used to measure two behavioral indicators, machine reading comprehension and question answering, and it is not linked to any constructs. The linked measurement claims concern these two behavioral indicators only.

**Space of uses.** No other measurement instrument is linked to NewsQA. Where papers describe its use, they include it among evaluated datasets for answering questions from long news passages, sometimes as the MRQA version, and one paper uses it specifically for the news articles domain with CNN article question-answer pairs.
