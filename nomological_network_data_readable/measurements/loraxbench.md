# LoraxBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  
**Also known as:** LORAXBENCH  

## State of the Field

A multitask, multilingual benchmark suite covering 20 Indonesian languages and 3 register variations across 6 tasks: reading comprehension, open-domain QA, natural language inference, causal reasoning, cultural QA, and machine translation.
