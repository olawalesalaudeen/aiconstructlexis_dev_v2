# seqBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A tunable benchmark for sequential reasoning in LLMs, featuring synthetic pathfinding tasks with orthogonal control over logical depth, backtracking count, and noise ratio, enabling fine-grained analysis of reasoning failure modes.
