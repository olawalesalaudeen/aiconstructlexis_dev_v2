# LaMP-QA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A benchmark for evaluating personalized long-form question answering, covering questions from Arts & Entertainment, Lifestyle & Personal Development, and Society & Culture, using user profiles and aspect-based evaluation derived from question narratives.
