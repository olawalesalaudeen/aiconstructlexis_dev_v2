# M4
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 8 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe M4 in two distinct ways. Some definitions present it as a short-term time series forecasting benchmark containing marketing data at different sampling frequencies, and one snippet describes it as a testbed with a collection of marketing data in different sampling frequencies. Several source snippets instead use M4 for a machine-generated text detection dataset or benchmark: documents generated by multiple LLMs in five domains, a multilingual resource with languages such as Chinese, Russian, Urdu, Indonesian, Arabic, and Bulgarian, and in some cases the Urdu subset or a large-scale multi-domain benchmark for cross-domain scenarios.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use M4 to measure two behavioral indicators, AI-generated text detection and time series forecasting, and no constructs are linked to it. The recorded measurement links are split across these two behavioral indicators: one paper reports AI-generated text detection as measured by the Urdu subset of M4, and another reports time series forecasting as measured in work on transfer learning between the M3 and M4 competitions.

**Space of uses.** Across the papers, M4 is positioned either as an evaluation benchmark or as a testbed. In the text-detection usage, papers use it for general evaluation, for constructing cross-domain scenarios, for incorporating multiple languages, and in one case for augmentation and retraining on the Urdu subset; in the time-series usage, it is chosen as the testbed. No measurement instrument is linked to it, though one paper evaluates its approaches using LWD as well as M4.
