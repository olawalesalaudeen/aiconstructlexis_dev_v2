## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across these papers, AggreFact is most commonly described as a benchmark dataset for evaluating factual consistency in summarization. Definitions portray it as providing fine-grained annotations and summary-level labels across multiple domains, while a narrower description presents it as a summary evaluation benchmark built around the CNN and XSum datasets. One paper also refers to the AGGREFACT-FTSOTA split, indicating use of a named split or subset.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use AggreFact to evaluate factual consistency in summarization, and some also frame it more broadly as a benchmark for summary evaluation. The recorded links do not connect it to any construct or behavioral indicator, so the explicit measurement claims here do not extend beyond those benchmark descriptions.

**Space of uses.** The available snippets position AggreFact as one benchmark used alongside other summarization evaluation datasets, especially TRUE and SUMMAC, in model testing. Papers also use it with specific summarization datasets, describing it as consisting of or evaluating CNN or CNN/DM and XSum, and one paper specifies evaluation on the AGGREFACT-FTSOTA split.
