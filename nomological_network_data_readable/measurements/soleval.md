# SolEval
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

Repository-level benchmark for Solidity smart contract generation, consisting of 1,507 real-world function samples from 28 repositories, with human-annotated requirements, contextual dependencies, and test cases to assess functional correctness, gas efficiency, and vulnerability rates.
