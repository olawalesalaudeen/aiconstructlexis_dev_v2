**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** The available definition treats MAGE as a supervised fine-tuned model for AI text detection. It is described as classifying text as human or machine-generated through binary classification on linguistic features.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers do not link MAGE to any constructs or behavioral indicators, so the available evidence does not specify broader concepts it is used to measure.

**Space of uses.** The available usage evidence places MAGE among other AI text detectors rather than as a standalone protocol. In Beyond Checkmate: Exploring the Creative Choke Points for AI Generated Texts, it is evaluated as part of a suite of prominent detectors alongside GPT-Zero, Radar, Binoculars, and GPT-Who.
