# Humor Style Questionnaire
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

Standardized 32-item psychological assessment measuring four humor styles—affiliative, self-enhancing, aggressive, and self-defeating—using a 5-point Likert scale, used here to probe LLMs' representation of humor-related constructs.
