# WikiAnn
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** WikiANN  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed work, papers refer to it as WikiAnn or WikiANN and most commonly treat it as a multilingual named entity recognition dataset. Definitions differ on scope and framing: some describe 282-language coverage and its use for low-resource Sino-Tibetan languages, with one survey noting that all ST languages other than Chinese amount to 131K name mentions, while other definitions describe a Wikipedia-extracted NER dataset for crosslingual sequence labeling and report 176-language coverage.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Claims about what it measures converge on a single behavioral indicator rather than spreading across multiple outcomes. Papers link it to 1 behavioral indicator, Named entity recognition, and to no constructs; correspondingly, it is repeatedly described as a dataset used for named entity recognition evaluation.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked alongside it. Papers use it as a direct evaluation set, including crosslingual sequence labeling evaluation and additional experiments on low-resource Sino-Tibetan languages; as one paper states, "We use WikiANN for named entity recognition (NER)" (LangSAMP: Language-Script Aware Multilingual Pretraining).
