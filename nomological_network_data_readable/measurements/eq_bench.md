# EQ-Bench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe EQ-Bench in two different ways. One treats it as a benchmark for creative writing, specifically a creative writing leaderboard scored by other LLMs. Another describes it as an emotional intelligence benchmark that incorporates human survey ratings and allows multiple correct answers through cosine similarity to weighted choice distributions.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across papers, EQ-Bench is linked to three constructs and no behavioral indicators. Those measurement claims are spread across emotion understanding, emotional intelligence, and social and emotional intelligence: papers report using it to evaluate emotional understanding ability, treat it as a benchmark from a similar emotional-intelligence domain, and place it within socio-emotional evaluation.

**Space of uses.** Papers use EQ-Bench as an evaluation benchmark. No measurement instrument is linked to it, though one paper groups it with LM-Pragmatics, SocialIQA, and SimpleToM under a socio-emotional category, and another uses it as a comparison point alongside other benchmarks from a similar domain.
