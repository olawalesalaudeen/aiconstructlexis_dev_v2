# TurnBack
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A large-scale benchmark for evaluating the route-level geospatial cognition of LLMs, consisting of a dataset of 36,000 routes, a language-to-geometry converter, and a comprehensive evaluation framework for the route reversal task.
