# PlanBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited definitions, the prevailing framing is as a benchmark for evaluating planning in LLMs. One definition narrows this to planning and Reasoning about Actions and Change, while another describes PlanBench as using Planning Domain Description Language problems converted into natural language to evaluate planning and reasoning abilities.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link PlanBench to one construct and no behavioral indicators, and the linked construct is Planning. The measurement claims therefore converge on planning rather than spreading across multiple targets, with one paper citing PlanBench among specialized benchmarks used in discussions of complex planning or multi-step reasoning challenges.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked to it. In the papers that mention it, PlanBench is used chiefly as a comparison point for other benchmarks, including side by side contrasts with prior Reasoning about Actions and Change benchmarks, and it is also grouped with closely related work that converts Planning Domain Description Language problems into natural language.
