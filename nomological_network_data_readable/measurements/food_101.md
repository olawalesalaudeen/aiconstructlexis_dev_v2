# Food-101
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 14 papers  
**Also known as:** Food101  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Most papers describe Food-101 as a food image classification dataset or benchmark organized around 101 food categories. Several definitions also frame it as a fine-grained image classification or recognition resource, and some add that it contains 101,000 images and is challenging. Some papers describe it more specifically as being used for zero-shot class descriptions, zero-shot transfer, or few-shot learning performance, while others use it more generally for image classification and prediction-correctness evaluation.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link Food-101 to 1 behavioral indicator and no constructs. The reported relationship is consistent: papers use Food-101 to measure Image classification, and no other behavioral indicators are linked to it. The measurement claims therefore converge on a single behavioral target rather than spreading across multiple constructs.

**Space of uses.** Papers usually place Food-101 within larger benchmark suites rather than using it alone, often alongside CIFAR-100, CUB, Caltech101, OxfordPets, and SUN397. The reported settings include fine-tuning, transfer learning, zero-shot and linear-probe evaluation, and fine-grained image recognition, with several papers presenting it as one benchmark among broader vision task collections. No measurement instrument is explicitly linked to it.
