**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, MFRC is described as the Moral Foundations Reddit Corpus, a dataset of 16,123 English Reddit posts from 12 subreddits, categorized into US politics, French politics, and everyday moral life. Another description presents it as a multi-label moral foundation corpus covering six moral foundations, with three annotators assigned to each sample, so the prevailing usage combines a Reddit post collection with a multi-label annotation setup.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use MFRC to measure one behavioral indicator, Moral foundation classification, and no constructs are linked to it. The measurement claim converges on that single use: papers describe MFRC as a corpus of six moral foundations and, in one definition, as an evaluation resource for subjective multi-label classification and distribution alignment.

**Space of uses.** Papers do not link MFRC to any other measurement instrument. The available descriptions present it as a Reddit-based, multi-label corpus, and one description notes that each sample received three annotators.
