**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers most commonly describe ReasonSeg as a benchmark dataset for reasoning segmentation, designed to evaluate segmentation based on complex reasoning. Some descriptions narrow it to an image reasoning segmentation benchmark used to assess reasoning capability on images, while another describes it as a referring expression segmentation benchmark used to assess pixel-level grounding. In use, authors also call it a challenging dataset and report CIoU, GIoU, and gIoU results on it.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the linked relationships, papers use ReasonSeg to measure one behavioral indicator, Reasoning segmentation, and no constructs are linked to it. This is the prevailing usage: papers say it evaluates the model's reasoning ability and use it to assess reasoning capability on images. The linked claims therefore converge on a single behavioral indicator rather than spreading across many constructs or indicators.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it as an evaluation benchmark and report performance with CIoU, GIoU, and gIoU. No measurement instrument is linked to it, and the snippets mainly show it being used to compare model performance, including improvements over another model and a reported gIoU result on the dataset.
