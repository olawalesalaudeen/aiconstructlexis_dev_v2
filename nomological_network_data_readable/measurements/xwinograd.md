# XWinograd
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** Xwinograd  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed definitions, XWinograd is described consistently as a multilingual or cross-lingual benchmark or task derived from the Winograd Schema Challenge. Papers frame it as testing pronoun resolution, with one definition presenting it as a benchmark for commonsense reasoning and another as a task for coreference and syntactic understanding in diverse languages; one paper also refers specifically to Chinese subsets.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use XWinograd to measure one construct and one behavioral indicator. The linked construct is Commonsense reasoning, including use of Chinese subsets in Chinese evaluation, and the linked behavioral indicator is Coreference resolution, with one paper describing XWinograd as a cross-lingual adaptation of the Winograd Schema Challenge for coreference resolution evaluation.

**Space of uses.** Papers do not link XWinograd to another measurement instrument. In use, it appears as an evaluation task, including a listing among discriminative tasks and a Chinese evaluation setup alongside XStoryCloze, XNLI, and XCOPA.
