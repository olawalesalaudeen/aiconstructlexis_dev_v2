# JudgeLM
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers treat JudgeLM as a judge model for automated evaluation of LLM responses, with outputs assessed either by scoring or by selecting among responses. They also mention model variants JudgeLM-7B, JudgeLM-13B, and JudgeLM-33B.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers do not explicitly present JudgeLM as measuring any behavioral indicator, and no behavioral indicators are linked to it. It is linked to one construct, Generalization, but that link is recorded as related rather than measured_by, so papers do not clearly frame JudgeLM as a measure of a named construct.

**Space of uses.** Papers place JudgeLM alongside PandaLM and GPT-4 as one of several judge models used for automated evaluation. The stated use is to evaluate LLM responses by scoring them or selecting outputs.

**Concerns raised.** One paper relates JudgeLM to Generalization and reports that judge models demonstrate poor generalization when evaluating different LLMs and benchmarks.
