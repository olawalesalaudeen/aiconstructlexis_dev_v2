# MAMO
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** Mamo  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, the prevailing usage is as a benchmark for mathematical or optimization modeling. Some descriptions add that it benchmarks modeling with code solvers and focuses on linear programming problems, while others present it through EasyLP and ComplexLP, either as two optimization datasets or as subsets of varying difficulty.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use MAMO to evaluate mathematical modeling, mathematical optimization modeling, or LLM performance on those tasks. No constructs or behavioral indicators are linked to it in the surveyed papers.

**Space of uses.** In the surveyed papers, MAMO appears as one benchmark within broader evaluation suites. It is used alongside NL4OPT, IndustryOR, OptiBench, and OptMATH-Bench in one evaluation setup, and another study includes Mamo, specifically EasyLP and ComplexLP, among real-world optimization and operation task datasets; one snippet also places it among established modeling benchmarks.
