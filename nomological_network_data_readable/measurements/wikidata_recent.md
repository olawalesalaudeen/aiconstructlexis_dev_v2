# WikiData_recent
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** WikiDatarecent  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe it as a dataset for knowledge editing evaluation that focuses on recent, updated facts from Wikidata. A paper also places it among datasets used in experiments involving knowledge insertion and knowledge modification.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use it to measure knowledge editing, and they also report it as measuring editing locality and linguistic quality. The linked claims cover one behavioral indicator, knowledge editing, and two constructs, editing locality and linguistic quality; in one table the construct-level uses appear under Locality and Fluency.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it alongside WikiDatacounterfact, WikiBio, ConvSent, and ZsRE when editing different kinds of knowledge, and another paper includes it in experiments involving knowledge insertion and knowledge modification. The papers do not link it to any other measurement instrument.
