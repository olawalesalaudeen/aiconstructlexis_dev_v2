# SyntaxGym
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

A suite of diagnostic tests evaluating six distinct syntactic phenomena through surprisal-based metrics, where models are assessed on their ability to assign appropriate surprisal to sentence continuations based on syntactic constraints.
