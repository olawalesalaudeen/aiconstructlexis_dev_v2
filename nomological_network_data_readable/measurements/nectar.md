**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** Berkeley Nectar  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers treat Nectar as a dataset used in evaluation. The fullest description presents it as containing human-labeled responses in seven distinct rankings and using those responses to evaluate question answering performance; one study says it "includes human-labeled responses categorized into seven distinct rankings." Some authors use the name Berkeley Nectar and describe it more narrowly as a dataset used in direct and indirect transfer evaluations of decoding-time alignment, without adding further content details.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the papers, the measurement claims are concentrated rather than spread across many targets. Nectar is linked to no constructs, to one behavioral indicator, Question answering, and to no measurement instruments. In the recorded relationship, Question answering is measured by Nectar, and the same line of work defines the dataset as being used to evaluate question answering performance.

**Space of uses.** Papers describe using Nectar in experiments and evaluations. One paper says it began experiments with the Nectar dataset, and another uses Berkeley Nectar in direct and indirect transfer evaluations of decoding-time alignment; that analysis uses Berkeley Nectar alongside UltraFeedback, while papers do not link it to any measurement instrument.
