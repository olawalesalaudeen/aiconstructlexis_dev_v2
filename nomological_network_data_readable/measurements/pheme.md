# PHEME
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe PHEME as a benchmark dataset for fake news detection made up of tweets related to five major news events. One definition also specifies its split and scale, stating that it contains 1,414 training tweets and 608 testing tweets.

**What it is claimed to measure.** In the recorded uses, papers use PHEME to measure misinformation detection. It is linked to 1 behavioral indicator and no constructs, so the reported measurement claim is concentrated on that single behavioral indicator rather than spread across multiple outcomes.

**Space of uses.** Across the cited studies, PHEME is used alongside Twitter and Weibo in one evaluation setup, and alongside Weibo21 and LLM-mis in another. Papers position it as one of several public or widely-used benchmark datasets for experiments, rather than as a standalone evaluation protocol.
