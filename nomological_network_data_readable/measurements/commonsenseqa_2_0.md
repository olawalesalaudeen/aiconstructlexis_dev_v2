# CommonsenseQA 2.0
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** CSQA2  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited work, CommonsenseQA 2.0 is treated as a dataset for commonsense reasoning. Definitions converge on that framing, with one study describing CSQA2 as containing "a large number of questions about entity knowledge and commonsense reasoning" (UniCoTT: A Unified Framework for Structural Chain-of-Thought Distillation), and another describing it as a multiple-choice dataset used to evaluate commonsense reasoning capabilities.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link it to two constructs and no behavioral indicators. The prevailing use is to measure Commonsense reasoning, with papers evaluating on it as one of several commonsense QA datasets; a smaller use links it to Factuality, where one paper places CSQA2 within a "Factual Reasoning Task."

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked to it. In the available papers, it is used within broader evaluation suites: one study evaluates on it alongside CREAK and StrategyQA, and another includes it among widely utilized commonsense QA datasets alongside CommonsenseQA, OpenBookQA, and ARC-Easy.
