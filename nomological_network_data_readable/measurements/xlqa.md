# XLQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A benchmark for locale-aware multilingual open-domain question answering, containing 3,000 English seed questions expanded into eight languages with human-verified annotations distinguishing locale-invariant and locale-sensitive cases.
