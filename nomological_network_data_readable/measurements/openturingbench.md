# OpenTuringBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A large-scale benchmark (>500K texts) for evaluating machine-generated text detection and authorship attribution, featuring diverse OLLMs and challenging tasks including out-of-domain texts, unseen models, and human-machine mixing.
