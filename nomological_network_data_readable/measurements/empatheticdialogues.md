# EmpatheticDialogues
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** Empathetic Dialogues, EmpathicDialogues, Empathic Dialogues, E-Dialogue  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited definitions, the prevailing treatment is as a dialogue dataset centered on emotional situations. Papers describe it as conversations grounded in specific emotional situations and, more specifically in one definition, as 24,850 multi-turn speaker-listener conversations annotated with one of 32 emotions for fine-grained emotion classification. Another naming variant in the papers, E-Dialogue, is described as a benchmark for evaluating empathetic capabilities in open-domain conversation models, so the descriptions emphasize either fine-grained emotion labels or empathetic evaluation.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use it most often as a task or evaluation dataset for fine-grained emotion classification and emotion detection in dialogue, while another definition says it evaluates empathetic capabilities in open-domain conversation models. No construct or behavioral indicator is linked to it, so the papers here describe its measurement role through tasks rather than explicit construct mappings.

**Space of uses.** Papers position it as a benchmark or task dataset. No measurement instrument is linked to it, but one paper reports using E-Dialogue alongside DialogSum, BeaverTails, HarmfulQA, and TruthfulQA to assess a module, while other papers invoke EmpatheticDialogues for fine-grained emotion detection or classification tasks.
