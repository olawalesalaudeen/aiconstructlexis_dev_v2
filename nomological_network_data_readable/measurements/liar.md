# LIAR
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the available definitions, papers treat LIAR as a dataset for evaluating claim verification. One paper also places it among "7 standard datasets" used for evaluation and describes its role here as assessing F1 score in true/false classification under prompt optimization.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The recorded relationships do not link LIAR to any construct or behavioral indicator as a measurement instrument. In the available descriptions, its stated evaluative role is claim verification and F1 assessment for true/false classification.

**Space of uses.** Papers use LIAR as one dataset within a broader evaluation setup alongside other standard datasets, including WSC, Ethos, and ArSarcasm. In the reported use, it appears in prompt optimization experiments, with one result summarized as "improving F1 score by 10.1 on LIAR dataset" (Efficient and Accurate Prompt Optimization: the Benefit of Memory in Exemplar-Guided Reflection).
