# MMC
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers most commonly describe MMC as a benchmark for evaluating chart understanding, with charts drawn from diverse sources. Another definition frames it as a recent benchmark for multimodal chart understanding and notes that some questions require external domain-specific knowledge.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link MMC to one behavioral indicator and to no constructs. The recorded measurement claim centers on Chart and diagram understanding, with one paper explicitly labeling MMC as chart understanding.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is formally linked to MMC. In practice, papers use or discuss it alongside other multimodal and chart benchmarks: one paper includes it in a broader benchmark suite and contrasts it with MMMU and MathVista by noting that those include mathematical figures, whereas MMC contains charts from diverse sources, and another discusses it alongside ChartBench and ChartX3 as a recent chart-understanding benchmark.

**Concerns raised.** Concerns appear in one paper, which says that some MMC questions require external domain-specific knowledge. The same paper also groups MMC with benchmarks that have issues with the source or diversity of charts and with the types of questions.
