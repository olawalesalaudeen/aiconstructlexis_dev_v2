## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers commonly describe WMT14 as a machine translation benchmark from the 2014 Workshop on Statistical Machine Translation, used to evaluate translation quality on standard translation directions. The descriptions vary in granularity: some refer to WMT14 generally, while others specify WMT14EnDe with training and test sets drawn from it, and relationship evidence also mentions French-English and English-French portions.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use WMT14 to measure Machine translation: it is linked to 1 behavioral indicator and to no constructs. The reported use is concentrated on that single indicator, with papers describing WMT14 as used for machine translation and for evaluating translation capabilities.

**Space of uses.** In use, papers present it as a direct translation evaluation, and some also place it in broader benchmark collections. No measurement instrument is linked to it, but one paper reports it alongside IWSLT14, afroMT, IWSLT17, WMT18, and WMT19 for machine translation, another includes WMT 2014 in a multilinguality grouping with two other benchmarks, and one relationship quote uses the English-French subset as part of a training set.
