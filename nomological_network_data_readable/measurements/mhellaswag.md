# MHellaSwag
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** MHELLASWAG, Okapi-HellaSwag  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe MHellaSwag, also referred to as MHELLASWAG or Okapi-HellaSwag, as a multilingual commonsense reasoning benchmark. The prevailing description presents it as sentences or paragraphs where models must choose the most likely continuation or ending, and one paper says it "aims at collecting the multilingual understanding abilities" (P-MMEval: A Parallel Multilingual Multitask Benchmark for Consistent Evaluation of LLMs). Another definition uses slightly different wording, framing Okapi-HellaSwag as evaluating event prediction and situational understanding.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the papers, claims converge on one linked construct: multilingual ability. Papers report it as measuring multilingual ability in evaluations of models' capability across languages, and no behavioral indicators are linked to it.

**Space of uses.** Papers position it as one benchmark within broader multilingual evaluation efforts rather than alongside any named companion instrument. One paper groups it with multilingual benchmarks that rely on English translation for cross-language evaluation, and no other measurement instrument is linked to it.
