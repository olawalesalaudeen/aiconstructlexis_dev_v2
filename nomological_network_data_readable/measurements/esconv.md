**Type:** Measurement

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers most commonly describe ESConv as a benchmark dataset for emotional support conversations. The available definition presents it as collected in a help-seeker and supporter mode, with approximately 1K conversations and 31K utterances, and with utterances annotated with one of eight support strategies.

**What it is claimed to measure.** In the linked work, ESConv is used to measure 1 behavioral indicator, Text generation, and it is not linked to any constructs. The reported measurement claim is narrow rather than spread across multiple outcomes: one paper says it conducts experiments on ESConv when applying IntentionFrame to enhance downstream response generation.

**Space of uses.** Across the cited work, ESConv is positioned as an experimental benchmark for emotional support conversation systems. No other measurement instrument is linked to it. Papers use it for experiments on response generation and framework evaluation, including studies that report experiments on ESConv when assessing emotional support performance and support strategy selection.
