# PUNMEMECN
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A multimodal benchmark for Chinese pun memes that assesses vision-language models on pun detection, sentiment analysis, and contextual meme response selection using annotated memes and simulated chat conversations.
