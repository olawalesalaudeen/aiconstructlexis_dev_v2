# MFQ
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** Moral foundations questionnaire  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, MFQ is described as the original Moral Foundations Questionnaire, a psychometric or psychological survey designed for humans. One definition says it measures how individuals evaluate abstract moral scenarios in relation to their own values, while another describes it as a survey that has been used to analyze which morals are most expressed in language model outputs.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use MFQ mainly in terms of moral evaluation and moral expression: one definition frames it as measuring evaluations of abstract moral scenarios relative to personal values, and another says it has been used to analyze which morals are most expressed in model outputs. The papers do not explicitly link it to any construct or behavioral indicator.

**Space of uses.** The usage described in the papers treats MFQ as a human psychological survey that is brought into LLM analysis. The papers do not report it being used alongside any other named measurement instrument.
