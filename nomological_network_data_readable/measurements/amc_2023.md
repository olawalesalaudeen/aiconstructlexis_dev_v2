## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the literature, AMC 2023 is most commonly treated as a benchmark or dataset drawn from the 2023 American Mathematics Competitions, sometimes written as AMC, AMC2023, or AMC23. Most definitions describe challenging, competition-level math problems at the high school level, with one definition presenting it as a 2023 exam of standardized math problems and another describing it as a middle school competition covering arithmetic, algebra, and geometry with 40 questions.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link AMC 2023 to 2 constructs and 1 behavioral indicator. The prevailing use is to measure Mathematical reasoning, with papers reporting it as a mathematical or olympiad benchmark for evaluating reasoning performance; a smaller line of work uses it to measure Mathematical problem solving, and one paper uses it to measure Reasoning. Overall, the measurement claims mostly converge on Mathematical reasoning rather than spreading evenly across several constructs.

**Space of uses.** Most papers use AMC 2023 as part of a broader benchmark suite rather than on its own. It is commonly reported alongside GSM8K, MATH500, AIME2024, OlympiadBench, and Omni-MATH, and papers variously position it as a competition-level benchmark, a challenging out-of-distribution exam, or a transfer test on challenging math questions; reported results include performance percentages, accuracy, and pass@k scores.
