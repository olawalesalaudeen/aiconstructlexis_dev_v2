**Type:** Measurement

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited papers, GossipCop is most commonly treated as a factuality-labeled dataset for entertainment and celebrity gossip content. The prevailing description is a collection of English-language celebrity gossip news articles, labeled for factuality or as true or false, and one definition also describes it as containing related social media posts alongside news articles.

**What it is claimed to measure.** In the recorded relationships, papers use GossipCop to measure one behavioral indicator, Misinformation detection, and no constructs are linked to it. The measurement claims converge on that single target: papers describe it as a benchmark for fake news or misinformation detection in entertainment-related domains.

**Space of uses.** Papers position GossipCop as a benchmark dataset for fake news detection tasks. One study presents it as the second of two benchmark datasets used to evaluate a framework, and another applies open-ended rewriting to fake news and rephrases real news from GossipCop alongside PolitiFact.
