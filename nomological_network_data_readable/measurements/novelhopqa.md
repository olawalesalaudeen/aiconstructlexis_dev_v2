# NovelHopQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  
**Also known as:** NOVELHOPQA  

## State of the Field

A benchmark for evaluating multi-hop question answering over long narrative contexts, featuring 4,000 questions with 1-4 reasoning hops across 64k-128k token excerpts from public-domain novels.
