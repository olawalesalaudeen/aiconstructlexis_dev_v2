# InfiniBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A large-scale benchmark for long-form video understanding in multi-modal models, assessing eight skills across 87.7K question-answer pairs from 1,000+ hours of movies and TV shows, using both multiple-choice and open-ended formats.
