# MEBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A benchmark comprising 4,780 question-answer pairs designed to evaluate LLMs and RAG systems on cross-document, multi-entity question answering across comparative, statistical, and relational reasoning tasks.
