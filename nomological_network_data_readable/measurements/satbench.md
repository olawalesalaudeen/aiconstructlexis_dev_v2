# SATBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A benchmark consisting of 2100 logical puzzles derived from SAT formulas, presented in natural language, used to evaluate models' ability to perform search-based logical reasoning and produce valid reasoning traces.
