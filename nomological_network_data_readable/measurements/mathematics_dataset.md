# Mathematics Dataset
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 7 papers  
**Also known as:** Mathematics, DeepMind-Mathematics  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Definitions usually treat Mathematics Dataset, also referred to as Mathematics or DeepMind-Mathematics, as a dataset or benchmark of mathematical problems for evaluating mathematical reasoning. The content is described as covering diverse mathematics topics, including algebra, arithmetic, calculus, and probability, with one description also listing comparison, measurement, and numbers; another says the problems are based on a national school mathematics curriculum up to age 16. A smaller variation in description is whether papers emphasize a curriculum-based generation process, a benchmark format, or a test set of 1000 problems.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use it to measure Mathematical reasoning. The measurement claims converge on one construct rather than spread across many: it is linked to one construct, Mathematical reasoning, and to no behavioral indicators.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it both as an out-of-domain evaluation dataset, alongside SVAMP and SimulEq, and as part of HUMANBENCH, which contains 7 datasets from the Mathematics Dataset. Another paper describes DeepMind-Mathematics directly as a test set.
