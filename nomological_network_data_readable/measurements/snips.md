# SNIPS
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Most descriptions treat SNIPS as a benchmark dataset for intent classification. One definition places it in a voice assistant setting with seven user intents and says it is used for evaluating task-oriented dialogue systems, while another presents it as a multi-domain dataset, mentioning restaurants, movies, and music, reporting 13,084 training utterances, and describing it as used to evaluate model reasoning on diverse and balanced data.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Claims about what SNIPS measures converge on a single behavioral indicator, intent classification. Papers use it to measure intent classification, and the linked measurement claims do not extend beyond that: it is linked to one behavioral indicator, Intent classification, and to no constructs.

**Space of uses.** Papers treat SNIPS as a benchmark used within evaluation setups rather than as a standalone protocol. One study pairs it with ATIS as one of two intent classification benchmark datasets, and another includes SNIPS as the intent classification dataset within a broader text classification comparison; papers do not otherwise link it to measurement instruments.
