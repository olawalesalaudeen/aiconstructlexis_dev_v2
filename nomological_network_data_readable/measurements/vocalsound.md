**What it is claimed to measure.** No constructs or behavioral indicators are linked to VocalSound. Instead, papers describe it more directly as evaluating whether models can recognize non-speech audio events and human vocal sounds beyond speech, so the reported usage converges on vocal sound recognition rather than a named construct.

**Space of uses.** Papers use VocalSound as an evaluation benchmark within vocal sound classification setups, rather than linking it to other named measurement instruments. In the available descriptions it appears as a benchmark entry and as an included dataset for testing recognition of non-verbal human vocalizations under varying acoustic conditions.
