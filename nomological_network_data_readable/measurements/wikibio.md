# WikiBio
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe WikiBio as a benchmark dataset for knowledge editing focused on biographical information. The available description does not specify versions or subsets, and it appears in a grouping of datasets used to edit different kinds of knowledge.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The papers link WikiBio to one behavioral indicator, Knowledge editing, through a measured_by relation, and no constructs are linked to it. The measurement claims therefore center on Knowledge editing rather than being spread across multiple constructs or indicators.

**Space of uses.** In Unlocking Efficient, Scalable, and Continual Knowledge Editing with Basis-Level Representation Fine-Tuning, WikiBio is used alongside WikiDatarecent, WikiDatacounterfact, ConvSent, and ZsRE, in the authors' setup for editing different kinds of knowledge. No measurement instrument is linked to it beyond this co-usage context.
