# TableEval
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A real-world benchmark for table question answering featuring complex table structures (hierarchical, nested, concise), multilingual content (Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English), and cross-domain data from finance, government, academia, and industry reports.
