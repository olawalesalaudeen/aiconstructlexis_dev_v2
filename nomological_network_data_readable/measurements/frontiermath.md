# FrontierMath
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe FrontierMath as a benchmark used to evaluate the complex problem-solving and reasoning abilities of large language models. It is also described as a new benchmark developed through a collaboration with over 60 expert mathematicians, containing around 300 problems across 23 categories.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the papers, FrontierMath is linked to two constructs, General ability and Reasoning, and no behavioral indicators. Papers use it to measure Reasoning, including one statement that it is used "to evaluate reasoning on several families of LLMs," and they also report it as measuring General ability.

**Space of uses.** Papers position FrontierMath as a reasoning benchmark. One study mentions "reasoning benchmarks, e.g. FrontierMath and ARC-AGI," so it appears alongside ARC-AGI in benchmark discussions, and no other measurement instrument is linked to it.
