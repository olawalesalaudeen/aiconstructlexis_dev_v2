# C3
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 7 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, C3 is most commonly treated as a Chinese reading comprehension benchmark or dataset, described both as Chinese Cloze-style Reading Comprehension and as a Chinese machine reading comprehension benchmark for evaluating reading comprehension in Chinese. A smaller variation in wording presents it as a modern Chinese reading comprehension dataset used to test the generalizability of a procedure, while other descriptions add that it covers Chinese passages and questions and is a free-form multiple-choice dataset.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Its measurement claims are concentrated rather than spread across many targets: papers link it to one behavioral indicator and one construct. One paper uses it to measure Machine reading comprehension, describing C3 as a free-form multiple-choice Chinese machine reading comprehension dataset, and another reports it as measuring Chinese language understanding through Chinese comprehension.

**Space of uses.** Papers use C3 as an evaluation benchmark or test set. One study explicitly marks it as a test benchmark, and another uses it to evaluate a procedure on a modern Chinese reading comprehension dataset to demonstrate that procedure's generalizability; no other measurement instrument is explicitly linked to it.
