## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the available definitions, Tatoeba is described as the validation split of a dataset for cross-lingual sentence retrieval. The only characterization provided is that papers use this split for independent validation of cross-lingual retrieval findings.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers do not link Tatoeba to any constructs or behavioral indicators as a measurement instrument. Instead, it is described only in relation to validating cross-lingual sentence retrieval results.

**Space of uses.** The available paper uses Tatoeba alongside the test split of BUCC-18, and positions it as an auxiliary validation check rather than describing any broader role. In the quoted usage, authors report that they present results on the validation split of Tatoeba "for an independent validation of our findings."
