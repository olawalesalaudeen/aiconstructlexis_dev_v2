**Type:** Measurement

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers most commonly describe ClothoAQA as an audio question-answering benchmark or dataset derived from Clotho for evaluating audio-language understanding. One definition gives more detail, describing 1,991 audio samples from the Clotho dataset, each paired with six crowdsourced questions and corresponding answers, and says it is used to evaluate natural language understanding of general audio content.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the papers here, its use converges on one target: Audio question answering is measured by ClothoAQA. Papers link it to one behavioral indicator, Audio question answering, and to no constructs; the definitions also describe it as evaluating audio-language understanding or natural language understanding of general audio content.

**Space of uses.** No other measurement instrument is linked to it. Papers instead use it as the audio question-answering evaluation, with one paper listing it among "foundational benchmarks" and another using it as the audio question-answering task in a broader audio evaluation setup.
