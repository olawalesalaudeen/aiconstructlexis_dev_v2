# IEMOCAP
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe IEMOCAP as the Interactive Emotional Dyadic Motion Capture database, used as a benchmark for emotion recognition from speech, and one paper further presents it as a multimodal dataset for emotion classification from spoken dialogue. In that usage, it is also used to evaluate text-based emotion detection in long conversational sequences. The cited evaluations also refer to IEMOCAP Session 5, and one paper specifies 1,241 utterances.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Among linked entities, papers connect it to one behavioral indicator, Emotion analysis, and to no constructs. The recorded relationship is that Emotion analysis is measured by IEMOCAP, with supporting snippets describing emotion recognition as being assessed on IEMOCAP and reporting accuracy for "ER | IEMOCAP Session 5". Its reported measurement role therefore centers on a single named behavioral indicator.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it as an evaluation benchmark, sometimes alongside other datasets. One study assesses emotion recognition on IEMOCAP and MELD, while another reports "consistent improvements on MultiWOZ 2.2 and IEMOCAP" and reports IEMOCAP accuracy gains over DEEPSEEK and MODERNBERT.
