## State of the Field

**What it is.** In the cited papers, CUAD is described as the Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset, a corpus of commercial legal contracts used to evaluate automated contract review and provision extraction. One characterization also presents it as a specialized contract understanding dataset with 510 labelled documents spanning 25 contract types.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link CUAD to two constructs and no behavioral indicators. It is reported as measuring contract understanding, and it is also linked to general ability, so the measurement claims are not confined to a single construct.

**Space of uses.** The cited papers do not link CUAD to any other measurement instrument. Where it is discussed, authors position it as a specialized dataset for contract understanding evaluation and for automated contract review and provision extraction.
