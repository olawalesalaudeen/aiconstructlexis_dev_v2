# FewRel
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Most commonly, papers treat FewRel as a benchmark dataset for few-shot relation extraction, used to evaluate model performance in low-resource relation classification settings. In continual relation extraction work, it is also described as an FCRE benchmark and is repeatedly paired with TACRED for method and baseline comparisons. A smaller framing describes it as a dataset for type discovery, focused on identifying relation types from text and grouped with FewEvent and FewNerd under information extraction tasks.

**What it is claimed to measure.** One paper reports FewRel as measuring relation extraction. In the surveyed work it is linked to one behavioral indicator, Relation extraction, and to no constructs, so the reported measurement claims center on that single indicator.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked to it. Even so, papers frequently use FewRel alongside TACRED when evaluating methods, and one paper explicitly describes FewRel and TACRED as "widely used benchmarks"; across these examples, it is positioned as a benchmark dataset for comparing methods and FCRE baselines.
