# WebQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed work, WebQA is most commonly described as a retrieval-augmented QA dataset for evaluating long-context retrieval performance. The available definition specifies 20 documents per question and frames the task around position-dependent accuracy; one paper also refers to it as "WebQA (WebQ)".

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use WebQA to measure Synthetic recall. Its reported measurement claims are concentrated rather than spread across concepts: it is linked to 1 behavioral indicator, Synthetic recall, and to no constructs.

**Space of uses.** In use, WebQA appears as part of a broader evaluation suite rather than a standalone protocol. One study says the evaluation "leverages" NaturalQuestions, TriviaQA, WebQA, and the specialized task KV Retrieval, and no other measurement instruments are linked to it.
