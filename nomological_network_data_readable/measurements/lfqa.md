# LFQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed papers, the dominant framing treats LFQA as a long-form question-answering dataset drawn from Reddit. Definitions say it was created by scraping Reddit questions and pairing them with human-written answers, and one paper further specifies that it is curated from posts and responses on Reddit's "Explain Like I'm Five" forum. A smaller framing presents LFQA as a pairwise comparison benchmark for evaluating long-form question answering.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers do not link LFQA to any constructs or behavioral indicators. Instead, they describe it at the task level, as being used for evaluating watermark robustness in question answering settings and for pairwise comparisons in long-form question answering.

**Space of uses.** Papers position it mainly as an evaluation set or prompt source. One paper says LFQA was adopted as prompts for a human paraphrasing study, while other definitions describe it as part of evaluation for watermark robustness and as an evaluation set for pairwise comparisons. No other measurement instruments are linked alongside it.
