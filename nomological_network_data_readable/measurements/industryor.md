**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, the prevailing description is of IndustryOR as an open-source dataset of commercial Operations Research problems. Definitions also frame it as the basis for a novel benchmark dataset for explainable OR, and one description specifies that it consists of 100 real-world optimization problems spanning linear, integer, and mixed-integer programming at various difficulty levels.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link IndustryOR to one construct, Accuracy, and no behavioral indicators are linked to it. The only recorded measurement claim is that Accuracy is measured by IndustryOR, with evidence described as formulation correctness results on benchmarks containing LPs and MILPs.

**Space of uses.** In the available papers, IndustryOR is positioned as an evaluation resource, either as the basis for a new benchmark dataset or as a benchmark of optimization problems at varied difficulty levels. No other measurement instruments are linked to it.
