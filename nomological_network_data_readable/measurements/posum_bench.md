# PoSum-Bench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A bilingual benchmark for evaluating positional bias in conversational summarization, covering English and French datasets across formal meetings, casual dialogues, and customer service interactions, with a reference-free evaluation framework based on semantic similarity.
