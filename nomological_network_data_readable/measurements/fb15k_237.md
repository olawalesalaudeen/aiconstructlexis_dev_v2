# FB15k-237
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 7 papers  
**Also known as:** FB15K-237  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the definitions, papers most commonly treat FB15k-237 as a benchmark dataset, or standard benchmark, for knowledge graph completion, derived from Freebase or from the original FB15k. The prevailing description is that it is a subset built by removing inverse relations to reduce or prevent test set leakage, and one definition adds that it focuses on common knowledge. A smaller line of work instead describes it as a large-scale knowledge graph with complex relational semantics.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use FB15k-237 to measure four behavioral indicators and do not link it to any constructs. It is most often used for link prediction, and is also reported as measuring fact checking, graph partitioning, and knowledge graph completion. Overall, the reported uses spread across multiple behavioral indicators rather than converging on a single linked construct.

**Space of uses.** Papers commonly use it alongside WN18RR, and some also pair it with YAGO3-10, PrimeKG, FB15k-237N, or the FEVER fact verification challenge. In these studies it is positioned as one of the benchmark datasets used for experimental evaluation, for example as one of "two large datasets" for link prediction or as a larger real-world graph used to assess scalability and practical utility.

**Concerns raised.** A recurring concern in the definitions is test set leakage: papers describe the benchmark as designed to prevent leakage from simple relation inversion, or as created by removing inverse relations that may lead to test set leakage.
