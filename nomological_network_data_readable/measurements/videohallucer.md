# VideoHallucer
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** In the available usage, papers treat VideoHallucer as a benchmark for evaluating video hallucination. The only explicit definition frames it as assessing whether model responses are consistent with video content, and it refers to Basic, Hallucinated, and Overall categories.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers describe VideoHallucer as evaluating video hallucination through whether generated responses stay consistent with the video content. Beyond that task description, papers do not explicitly link it to any named construct or behavioral indicator.

**Space of uses.** The available usage places VideoHallucer alongside EventHallusion rather than on its own. One study says it adopted two video hallucination benchmarks, VideoHallucer and EventHallusion, so in that use it functions as one of a paired set of video hallucination evaluations.
