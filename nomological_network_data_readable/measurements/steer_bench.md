# STEER-BENCH
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A benchmark for evaluating community-specific steerability in LLMs using contrasting Reddit communities, comprising over 10,000 instruction-response pairs and 5,500 multiple-choice questions across 30 subreddit pairs and 19 domains.
