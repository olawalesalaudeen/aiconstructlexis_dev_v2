# Fashion-MNIST
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  
**Also known as:** FashionMNIST, Fashion MNIST  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers most commonly describe Fashion-MNIST as a dataset of Zalando article, or clothing, images with 60,000 training examples and 10,000 test examples. The prevailing framing presents it as an image-classification dataset, while another definition describes it as a drop-in replacement for MNIST; papers also use both the "Fashion MNIST" and "FashionMNIST" spellings.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the reported relationships, papers use it to measure one behavioral indicator, Image classification, and they do not link it to any constructs. One paper records the directional claim that Image classification is measured by Fashion-MNIST, in a setting that considers models trained on MNIST and the Fashion-MNIST dataset of clothing images.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked to it. In the usage snippets, papers place it alongside MNIST: one uses MNIST for pretraining and FashionMNIST for fine-tuning, and another considers models trained on both MNIST and Fashion-MNIST.
