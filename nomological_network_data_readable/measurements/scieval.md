# SciEval
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed work, the prevailing usage treats SciEval as a benchmark for scientific-domain or scientific-research evaluation. Definitions describe it as measuring a model's ability to understand and reason about scientific content, and several descriptions further frame it as a multi-level benchmark for scientific research; one description also presents it as a comprehensive, multi-disciplinary benchmark for assessing scientific reasoning and subjective question-answering capabilities.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use SciEval primarily to measure Scientific reasoning: it is linked to one construct overall, Scientific reasoning, and to no behavioral indicators. This makes its reported measurement role concentrated rather than spread across many constructs, though the definitions also describe it as evaluating scientific research reasoning, problem solving, and subjective question-answering capabilities.

**Space of uses.** Papers position SciEval as a benchmark test within broader evaluation suites. One paper lists it alongside LegalBench, LawBench, MedQA, MedMCQA, and FinEval in a benchmark table, while another reports benchmark tests on SciEval, presenting it as one of the benchmark evaluations used in the study.
