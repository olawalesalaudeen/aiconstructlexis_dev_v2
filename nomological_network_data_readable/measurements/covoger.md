# CoVoGER
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A multilingual multitask benchmark for speech-to-text generative error correction, covering ASR and ST across 15 languages and 28 language pairs, using N-best lists from Whisper and SeamlessM4T models.
