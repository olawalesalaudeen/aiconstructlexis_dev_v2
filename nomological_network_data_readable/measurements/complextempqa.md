# ComplexTempQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  
**Also known as:** COMPLEXTEMPQA  

## State of the Field

A large-scale dataset of over 100 million temporal question-answer pairs used to evaluate temporal reasoning, with questions categorized into attribute, comparison, and counting types, and enriched with metadata such as time span and popularity.
