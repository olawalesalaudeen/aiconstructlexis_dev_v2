# CharXiv
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers most commonly describe CharXiv as a benchmark or evaluation suite for chart understanding and complex visual reasoning. Definitions present it as using real-world chart data from arXiv preprints, and one definition specifies 2,323 natural, challenging, and diverse charts with hand-curated descriptive and reasoning questions. Across these descriptions, some authors call it a benchmark dataset, while others call it a comprehensive evaluation suite.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use CharXiv to measure one construct and two behavioral indicators. The linked construct is reasoning, and the linked behavioral indicators are chart and diagram understanding and complex reasoning question answering. The relationship evidence also describes its reasoning questions as requiring synthesizing information across complex visual elements.

**Space of uses.** No other measurement instrument is linked to CharXiv. In use, papers report validation-set results on it to compare systems across domains, particularly on tasks involving complex visual reasoning and interpretation of charts and graphs, and one survey positions it as expanding chart-understanding scenarios through real-world chart data from arXiv preprints.
