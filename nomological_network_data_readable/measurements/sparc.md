# SPaRC
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** The available descriptions do not converge. In "SPaRC: A Spatial Pathfinding Reasoning Challenge," the name refers to a benchmark of 1,000 2D grid pathfinding puzzles used to evaluate spatial and rule-based reasoning under multiple constraints. In "Track-SQL: Enhancing Generative Language Models with Dual-Extractive Modules for Schema and Context Tracking in Multi-turn Text-to-SQL," SParC is described instead as a benchmark dataset for multi-turn Text-to-SQL, with 4,298 dialogue sequences and over 12,000 questions paired with SQL queries, used to evaluate cross-domain semantic parsing in interactive settings.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the recorded relationships, papers use it to measure 4 constructs and no behavioral indicators. The linked constructs are constraint satisfaction, multi-step planning, rule-based reasoning, and spatial reasoning, with the available evidence stating that the benchmark reveals limitations regarding all four.

**Space of uses.** Usage details are limited. No measurement instrument is linked to it in the recorded relationships, but a reported evaluation uses SParC alongside CoSQL as a benchmark dataset for validating a method.
