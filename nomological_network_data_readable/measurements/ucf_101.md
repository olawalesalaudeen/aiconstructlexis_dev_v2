# UCF-101
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 7 papers  
**Also known as:** UCF101  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed papers, UCF-101 is most commonly described as an action recognition video benchmark or dataset that is also used to evaluate video generation, especially in class-conditional or class-to-video settings. Definitions also present it as a dataset of human action videos, including realistic videos from YouTube or videos in the wild, and some papers extend its use to image-to-video generation or action classification. Papers further describe it as a common or standard benchmark in video generation experiments.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link UCF-101 to 1 construct and 2 behavioral indicators. The prevailing usage is as a measure of Video generation, while a smaller line of work uses it to measure Action recognition. For constructs, the links converge on Fidelity: one paper links it to Fidelity in a comparison where existing benchmarks are described as assessing visual quality and text relevance.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked to it. Even so, papers describe using it alongside Kinetics-600 and MSR-VTT in video generation experiments, and alongside ActivityNet and Something-Something V2 in action recognition evaluation. The surrounding language typically positions it as a common or standard benchmark.
