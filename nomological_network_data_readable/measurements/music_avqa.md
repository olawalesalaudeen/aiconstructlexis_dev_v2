# MUSIC-AVQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** MUSIC-QA  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe MUSIC-AVQA as a multimodal question answering benchmark over audio and video. One definition calls it an open-ended VideoQA dataset used for multimodal question answering, while another paper uses the name MUSIC-QA and frames it as a benchmark for music-related audio-visual understanding and reasoning.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across papers, it is linked to two behavioral indicators and no constructs. Papers use it to measure Audio-visual question answering and Video question answering, with one paper presenting fine-tuning results on Audio-Video Question Answering using MUSIC-AVQA and another listing it among open-ended VideoQA datasets.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is linked to it through the recorded relationships. In practice, papers evaluate with it alongside other benchmarks, for example with MSVD as an open-ended VideoQA dataset, and in a broader audio-visual QA suite that also includes AVSD, AVSSD, MSVD-QA, and MSRVTT-QA, along with other benchmarks.
