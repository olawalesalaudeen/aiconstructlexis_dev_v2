# JEEBench
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** JEEBENCH, JEE-Bench  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Most commonly, papers describe JEEBench as a mathematics benchmark or challenging dataset used to benchmark LLM performance on math problems. Some definitions are more specific, describing coverage of college-level topics such as simple ODEs and multivariable calculus, while another frames it as a collection of Indian Institute of Technology entrance exam problems assessing advanced mathematical reasoning. Papers also place it among more challenging, exam-style problem sets.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across the recorded links, the measurement claim converges on one construct: one paper uses JEEBench to measure Mathematical reasoning. It is linked to one construct and no behavioral indicators, so the reported measurement use here is concentrated rather than spread across multiple targets.

**Space of uses.** Papers position it as part of broader hard-math evaluation setups rather than as a standalone protocol description. One study includes JEE-Bench among hard tasks spanning from high school competition to college-level exams, alongside Olympic-Arena and MATH, and another treats it as an additional challenging dataset alongside OmniMATH; no measurement instrument is formally linked to it in the recorded relationships.

**Concerns raised.** Only limited concern language appears. One paper reports that on JEE-Bench, "accuracy fluctuates with rising error rates".
