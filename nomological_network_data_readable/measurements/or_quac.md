# OR-QuAC
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited definitions, OR-QuAC is treated as a conversational question answering dataset. The prevailing description emphasizes open-retrieval settings with conversational, multi-turn queries and associated passages, while another paper highlights that it includes context-independent rewrites of dialogue questions, allowing those questions to be posed without prior context and used in both dialogue-based and isolated-question evaluation.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers do not explicitly link OR-QuAC to any construct or behavioral indicator, so the available sources do not frame it as measuring a named construct. Instead, they describe it as an evaluation dataset for conversational QA, including use in dialogue-based settings and in settings where rewritten questions can be asked independently of earlier turns.

**Space of uses.** In the available papers, OR-QuAC appears as part of broader evaluation suites alongside QuAC, CoQA, and DoQA. One study includes it in the main evaluation on widely used conversational search datasets, and another uses it to assess generalization beyond the training distribution.
