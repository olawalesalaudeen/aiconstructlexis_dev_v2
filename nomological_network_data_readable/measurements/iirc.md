# IIRC
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 6 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited papers, IIRC is treated as a benchmark dataset for reading comprehension, expanded in one definition as Iterative Information Retrieval and Comprehension. A more specific description frames it as focused on information integration and reading comprehension, where models answer questions by linking information across document sections, testing retrieval and contextual understanding. One paper also reports evaluation on decontextualized versions of a subset of IIRC.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Claims about what IIRC measures are concentrated rather than spread across many targets. Papers link it to one behavioral indicator, Question answering, describing it as one of the QA datasets used for measurement, and to one construct, Mathematical reasoning, which a smaller line of work treats as measured by IIRC.

**Space of uses.** Papers use IIRC for evaluation, including as one of four QA datasets in one study and in decontextualized subset form in another, where it is used alongside DROP. The available descriptions also position it as a setting where methods are compared across metrics, with one paper reporting a consistent advantage on IIRC across metrics.
