# CondAmbigQA
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  

## State of the Field

A benchmark and dataset of 2,000 ambiguous questions with condition-answer-citation triples, designed to evaluate models' ability to resolve ambiguity through explicit condition identification and conditional answer generation.
