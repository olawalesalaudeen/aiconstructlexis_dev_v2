# DUDE
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 6 papers  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across papers, DUDE is most commonly described as a benchmark for multi-page document understanding, with documents limited to up to 20 pages. One paper describes it more specifically as a multi-page visual question answering dataset containing financial reports, and papers also refer to its diverse domains and complex document structures.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link DUDE to one construct, Long-context understanding, and no behavioral indicators are linked to it. The measurement claim is narrow rather than spread across many constructs: one paper reports that Long-context understanding is measured by DUDE, noting that the benchmark includes a small percentage of cross-page questions (2.1%) and unanswerable questions (12.7%).

**Space of uses.** Usage evidence shows DUDE being used alongside MPVQA, CUAD, and MOAMOB in a multi-dataset evaluation. In that example, papers use it to evaluate retrieval and QA performance, and beyond that co-usage example papers do not relate DUDE to any other named measurement instrument.

**Concerns raised.** Papers note a scope limit: DUDE is described as not assessing documents exceeding 20 pages. One paper also notes that the benchmark includes a small percentage of cross-page questions and unanswerable questions.
