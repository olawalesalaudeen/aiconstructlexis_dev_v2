# Wizard of Wikipedia
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 6 papers  
**Also known as:** WoW, Wizard-of-Wikipedia  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across definitions, papers describe Wizard of Wikipedia, also called WoW or Wizard-of-Wikipedia, as a knowledge-grounded or open-domain dialogue dataset or benchmark in which one conversational partner, the "wizard," has access to Wikipedia or Wikipedia articles to inform the conversation. The prevailing description is this shared setup, while some papers emphasize it as a benchmark for knowledge-grounded dialogue and others as a dialogue dataset used for training models.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Several papers report that Wizard of Wikipedia measures dialogue generation. It is linked to one behavioral indicator, Dialogue generation, and no constructs, so dialogue generation is the only reported measurement target.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it in multiple roles: for evaluation, for training dialogue models, and for dialogue analysis. They place it alongside HotpotQA for evaluation, and alongside DailyDialog and DialogSum for controlled response generation, grammar detection, and grammar skill co-occurrence analysis in adjacent speaker turns; no other measurement instrument is linked to it.
