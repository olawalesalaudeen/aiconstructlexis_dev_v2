# Chatbot Arena Conversations
**Type:** Measurement  
**Referenced in:** 6 papers  
**Also known as:** LMSys  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the available papers, it is most commonly described as the LMSys Chatbot Arena Conversations dataset, a collection of real-world user preferences gathered from user votes on pairwise comparisons of LLM outputs. Another paper describes it as a dataset of human-annotated pairwise model comparisons.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The papers do not link this dataset to any named constructs or behavioral indicators. In their own descriptions, they use it to capture real-world user preferences and to compute human agreement with LLM judge decisions.

**Space of uses.** One paper positions it as a usage dataset for broader real-world preferences, stating that "the usage datasets capture broader real-world preferences" (HumT DumT: Measuring and controlling human-like language in LLMs). Another uses it to compute human agreement and evaluates agreement on held-out test instructions not used for model selection; the papers do not link it to other measurement instruments.
