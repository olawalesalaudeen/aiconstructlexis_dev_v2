**Type:** Measurement

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, the prevailing description treats ImageNet-R as an image classification or out-of-distribution benchmark built from artistic renditions of ImageNet object classes. Definitions repeatedly mention forms such as paintings, cartoons, sculptures, embroidery, and graffiti, and one paper describes these renditions as domains; the benchmark is framed as testing generalization to novel styles, different artistic forms, or transformed visual appearances.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers most commonly present ImageNet-R as evaluating robustness to out-of-distribution images or out-of-distribution robustness, with adjacent descriptions of generalization to novel styles, different artistic forms, and transformed visual appearances. No constructs and no behavioral indicators are linked to it in the recorded relationships, so the available claims stay at the level of benchmark purpose rather than explicit construct or behavior mappings.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it mainly as an evaluation benchmark for out-of-distribution images and artistic renditions of ImageNet classes. Learning from Teaching Regularization: Generalizable Correlations Should be Easy to Imitate uses it alongside ImageNet-Sketch to investigate out-of-distribution robustness, and UniBench: Visual Reasoning Requires Rethinking Vision-Language Beyond Scaling describes it as focusing on transformations related to many types of image renditions.
