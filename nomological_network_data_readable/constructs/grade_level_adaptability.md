# Grade-level adaptability
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 1 paper  
**Also known as:** Developmental suitability  

## State of the Field

The latent ability of an LLM to adjust its responses in terms of language, complexity, and content to match the cognitive and developmental stage of students at different grade levels.
