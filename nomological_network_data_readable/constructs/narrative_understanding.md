# Narrative understanding
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** In the available definition, narrative understanding is framed as a latent ability: capturing and interpreting the details and implications of a story when producing or evaluating a summary. The same paper ties this ability closely to summarization, describing story summaries as a way to distill a narrative's details and takeaways and as a vehicle for examining how well the summarizer understands the story.

**Space of measurement.** No measurement instrument is linked to narrative understanding, and no behavioral indicator is linked to it. The only operational cue in the papers is story summarization itself, since the construct is discussed in the context of producing or evaluating summaries and summarization is described as a way to examine the understanding a summarizer has of the story.

**Links.** One paper relates causal reasoning to narrative understanding. In How LLMs Comprehend Temporal Meaning in Narratives: A Case Study in Cognitive Evaluation of LLMs, difficulty with causal reasoning derived from aspect is presented as raising concerns about the ability to fully comprehend narratives.
