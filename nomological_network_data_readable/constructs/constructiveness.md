# Constructiveness
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** In the available usage, constructiveness is framed as a property of feedback: the degree to which it identifies actionable weaknesses or improvement areas that can help authors revise a paper. The same source also characterizes it through "actionable feedback comments generation" and through "specific, critical feedback points" that target substantive weaknesses and improvement areas, so the emphasis stays on actionable, revision-oriented critique.

**Space of facets.** No formal subtypes, equivalent constructs, or contrasts are introduced. Instead, the term is elaborated with phrases such as "actionable feedback comments generation" and "specific, critical feedback points targeting substantive weaknesses and improvement areas in a paper," which restate the construct rather than dividing it into separate facets.

**Space of measurement.** Although the definition is phrased as "the degree to which" feedback identifies actionable weaknesses or improvement areas, no benchmark, human evaluation setup, LLM-as-a-judge procedure, probe, or analysis is linked to it as a measurement instrument. No behavioral indicator is linked to it, and no other constructs are linked to it through recorded measurements.
