# Narrative coherence
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** Story coherence, Plot coherence, Plot quality  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** Across the definitions, the prevailing usage treats the concept as coherence in a story or plot whose events and structure remain logically consistent and understandable. Most definitions emphasize plot-level organization and progression, describing a recognizable structure in which events move the narrative forward without logical or conceptual inconsistencies and follow a logical, causally sound sequence. A broader framing extends this beyond plot logic to overall story quality, where plot development, context, and expression stay consistent and readable.

**Space of facets.** Papers split the idea mainly between story-level and plot-level variants. One usage reserves story coherence for overall long-form quality, while plot coherence and plot quality refer more narrowly to event sequencing, recognizable structure, and connected narrative development. In that narrower usage, a coherent plot is described through completeness, logical progression, and turns that move the narrative forward.

**Space of measurement.** Narrative coherence is linked to one measurement instrument, LLM-as-a-judge, and to no behavioral indicators. The recorded operationalization is judge-based evaluation of model outputs: papers describe side-by-side comparisons with prompts targeting story-quality dimensions, and GPT-4 is used as a judge to score outputs on an overall score and several aspects, including story coherence. The measurement therefore takes the form of direct output ratings, while the definitions describe the target of those ratings in terms of coherent plot development, contextual consistency, logical progression, and readable expression.

**Links.** No papers here report causal, correlational, or other related links for narrative coherence. The only recorded relationship is methodological: papers state that it is measured by LLM-as-a-judge.
