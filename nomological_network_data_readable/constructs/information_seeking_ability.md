# Information-seeking ability
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  
**Also known as:** Information seeking  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Consensus. Most papers define information-seeking ability as a model's active ability to identify missing information and ask effective questions to gather what it needs under uncertainty. One paper instead uses the term for answering factual or explanatory queries, which is a different phenomenon, but it appears to be a lone outlier rather than the main pattern.

**Space of definitions.** Across the papers, the prevailing usage treats the term as a model-side ability to notice missing knowledge and actively gather what is needed by asking questions before answering, especially under uncertainty or ambiguity. In this framing, the construct is tied to proactive questioning, eliciting missing details, and judging whether enough information has been collected to give a confident answer. A smaller line of work uses the same label for a task type instead: responding to queries that ask for factual information or explanations, rather than a latent capability of the model.

**Space of facets.** The papers mainly organize the concept around question asking and active information gathering rather than around named subtypes. Within the dominant framing, it is described alongside question-asking ability and active information-seeking capabilities, and one paper characterizes success as deciding at each turn whether more information is needed before giving a confident answer. Across papers, the term therefore covers both a latent model property and, in a minority usage, a class of information-seeking questions.

**Space of measurement.** Operationalization is sparse: papers link it to one measurement instrument, AlpacaEval, and no behavioral indicators or constructs are linked to it. The linked evidence describes AlpacaEval as mostly focusing on information-seeking tasks, so the recorded measurement is expressed in terms of task content labeled information-seeking. This differs in emphasis from the dominant definitions, which describe proactive question-asking to elicit missing details.

**Links.** The only explicit relationship reported is that it is measured by AlpacaEval, with one paper describing that instrument as mostly focused on information-seeking tasks. No papers report causal, correlational, or other related links for this construct.
