# Morphosyntactic competence
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 2 papers  
**Also known as:** Morpho-syntactic competence  

## State of the Field

The model's integrated ability to process and understand both morphological (word structure) and syntactic (sentence structure) information in context.

## Sources

**[Extracting Linguistic Information from Large Language Models: Syntactic Relations and Derivational Knowledge](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1384.pdf)**
> The model's integrated ability to process and understand both morphological (word structure) and syntactic (sentence structure) information in context.

**[Exploring morphology-aware tokenization: A case study on Spanish language modeling](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1552.pdf) (as "Morpho-syntactic competence")**
> The latent ability of a model to capture syntactic dependencies governed by morphological features such as gender, number, and person agreement in a fusional language.
