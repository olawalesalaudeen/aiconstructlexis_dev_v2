# Judge sentiment bias
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** Sentiment bias  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** Across the available definition, the construct is framed as an LLM judge's tendency to have its judgment affected by emotional expression in the content it evaluates. The paper describes this as a preference for positive or negative emotions, and its source language also states that the judge may prefer content without emotional elements.

**Space of measurement.** No measurement instrument is linked to this construct, and no behavioral indicator is linked to it. The available papers therefore do not specify an operationalization for it.

## Sources

**[Justice or Prejudice? Quantifying Biases in LLM-as-a-Judge](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/fdca08d371e4b6c031397909e20043bd-Paper-Conference.pdf) (as "Sentiment bias")**
> A tendency for an LLM judge to prefer or be influenced by positive or negative emotional expression in the content being judged.
