# Implicit social bias
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 2 papers  
**Also known as:** Implicit demographic bias  

## State of the Field

The tendency of a model to exhibit unconscious, automatic attitudes or stereotypes that influence its decisions and behaviors without being explicitly directed to do so.
