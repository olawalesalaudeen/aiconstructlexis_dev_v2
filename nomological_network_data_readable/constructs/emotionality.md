# Emotionality
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** Across the available usage, Emotionality is framed as a latent tendency of an LLM to express emotional sensitivity and vulnerability. In Exploring the Impact of Personality Traits on LLM Bias and Toxicity, this framing is explicitly tied to generated text: both high and low Emotionality scores are described as leading to an increase in negative responses, and responses are said to "tend to be more negative" regardless of whether the score is high or low.

**Space of measurement.** Papers do not link Emotionality to any measurement instrument or behavioral indicator.
