# Warmth
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** Across the surveyed work, warmth is treated as a social or interpersonal quality, but the exact emphasis differs. One framing defines it as the extent to which a chatbot conveys empathy and emotional connection during interaction, including a reduced form as whether the chatbot conveys empathy. Another framing treats warmth as the perceived intent or social friendliness dimension of an impression target, for example whether it appears benevolent, friendly, hostile, combative, or friendly. The main disagreement is whether warmth is described as conveyed empathy in interaction or as a broader impression dimension centered on perceived intent and friendliness.

**Space of facets.** The available systematization is limited. One paper places warmth in a two-dimensional impression framework alongside competence, and in that usage warmth is the perceived intent dimension of the impression target. Beyond that pairing, no linked constructs are recorded.

**Space of measurement.** Warmth is linked to 0 measurement instruments and 0 behavioral indicators. The only concrete uses described in the papers are that one reports positive ratings on warmth among chatbot characteristics, and another treats warmth as a dimension in trait impressions.
