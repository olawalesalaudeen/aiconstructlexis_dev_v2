# Semantic reasoning
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 12 papers  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** Across the available definitions, semantic reasoning is consistently treated as a model ability, most often as understanding the meaning of language instructions and their relationship to objects and actions in an environment. Another framing treats it as latent multi-step inference over concepts not explicitly stated in text, used to connect implicit content and identify implicit targets such as "democracy." The disagreement is mainly about scope: grounded instruction meaning versus unstated textual meaning.

**Space of facets.** Papers do not provide a shared internal breakdown of the construct. One taxonomy places semantic as one of six subtypes inside a broader text-based reasoning category within natural language reasoning. Elsewhere, semantic reasoning is mentioned together with visual reasoning and with spatial reasoning, rather than broken into further facets.

**Space of measurement.** No measurement instrument is linked to semantic reasoning. It is linked to 2 behavioral indicators, Referring expression understanding and Table question answering: the former is described as localizing an object from a complex textual query that requires spatial and semantic reasoning, and the latter as a Table QA setting involving complex semantic reasoning and free-form responses. In these papers, the construct is invoked at the task level rather than tied to a named instrument.

**Links.** Papers report only related links, not causal or correlational ones. Referring expression understanding is related to semantic reasoning in work on object localization from complex textual queries, semantic reasoning is related to table question answering in work on Table QA with complex semantic reasoning and free-form responses, and natural language reasoning is related to semantic reasoning in a text-based reasoning taxonomy where semantic appears as one subtype.
