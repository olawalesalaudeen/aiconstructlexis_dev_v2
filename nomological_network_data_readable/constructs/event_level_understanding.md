# Event-level understanding
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 2 papers  
**Also known as:** Event-centric analysis  

## State of the Field

The ability to comprehend videos at a fine-grained level by identifying, localizing, and describing individual events, as opposed to providing a single summary for the entire video.

**[E.T. Bench: Towards Open-Ended Event-Level Video-Language Understanding](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/38ace89a980ead30c6be2b46afc1a5bd-Paper-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.pdf)**

**[SVBench: A Benchmark with Temporal Multi-Turn Dialogues for Streaming Video Understanding](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/693ce820ea7e0f0c70a2a833cbdf7ccc-Paper-Conference.pdf) (as "Event-centric analysis")**
