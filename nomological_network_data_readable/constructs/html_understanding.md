# HTML understanding
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  
**Also known as:** HTML comprehension, Structural awareness  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Consensus. All papers define HTML understanding as a model's ability to comprehend the structure and semantics of HTML or document elements, often including hierarchy, content, or functionality. The differences are only in emphasis, such as informing actions or improving representations, so they are studying the same core capability.

**Space of definitions.** Across the surveyed work, the prevailing usage treats HTML understanding as an ability to comprehend HTML structure and semantics. Most definitions add either the content of HTML documents or the functionality of HTML elements, and one explicitly frames this understanding as informing actions. A smaller line of work uses a broader neighboring framing, structural awareness, as the ability to comprehend and utilize hierarchical and semantic information in document structures such as HTML tags to improve representation quality.

**Space of facets.** The available definitions organize the construct mainly through recurring components rather than explicit subtypes: structure, semantics, and then either content, functionality, or hierarchical information. "HTML comprehension" and "HTML understanding" are used for closely matching abilities, while "structural awareness" shifts the emphasis toward using structural cues in representations. In one paper, it is discussed as a distinct capability alongside grounded multimodal perception and multi-step reasoning.

**Space of measurement.** The construct is linked to 1 measurement instrument and 1 behavioral indicator overall. The only named instrument is WebSRC, which one paper describes as "a static HTML comprehension benchmark," so the operationalization here is benchmark-based and stays close to the dominant definition's focus on HTML comprehension. The linked behavioral indicator appears as a causal target rather than as an explicit measure.

**Links.** Beyond measurement, one paper claims a causal relationship from HTML understanding to time series forecasting; the quoted evidence is phrased in terms of improving "awareness of logic and structure." No correlational or generic related links are recorded, and no linked constructs are listed.
