# Coarse perception
**Type:** Construct  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**Definition agreement:** Too little evidence.

**Space of definitions.** The only explicit definition treats coarse perception as a latent ability to perceive and describe high-level, general elements within an image. The same paper also describes models as showing "strong coarse and fine-grained perceptual abilities" and being "equally adept at handling coarse perception contents."

**Space of facets.** The published usage places coarse perception within a perceptual capability space alongside fine-grained perception. One source describes models as having "coarse and fine-grained perceptual abilities," and MMBench abbreviates the category as CP while listing it next to fine-grained perception and several reasoning categories.

**Space of measurement.** Papers operationalize coarse perception with 2 measurement instruments, MMBench and MMStar, and it is not linked to any behavioral indicator. In MMBench, it appears explicitly as the CP category in a capability table, while MMStar is described as benchmarking 6 core capabilities and 18 detailed axes.

**Links.** Beyond being measured by MMBench and MMStar, the papers here do not claim causal, correlational, or other related-construct links for coarse perception.
