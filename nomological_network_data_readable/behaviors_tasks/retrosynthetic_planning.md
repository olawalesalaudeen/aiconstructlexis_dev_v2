# Retrosynthetic planning
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 7 papers  
**Also known as:** Retrosynthesis, Reaction prediction  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the surveyed work, the dominant framing treats this behavior as working backward from a target molecule or compound to identify precursor or reactant molecules for synthesis. Some definitions make the output a full, step-by-step synthesis pathway ending in commercially available reactants, while a narrower formulation treats success as predicting a one-step retrosynthetic reaction or reaction template from a product molecule and text context.

**Variants.** The named variants in the papers are retrosynthetic planning, retrosynthesis, and reaction prediction. They are formulated as step-by-step pathway generation for a designed or target molecule, identification of potential reactants for a target compound, and one-step template prediction from a product molecule and text context, with predicted templates used as reaction nodes.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** Papers link this behavior to 1 measurement instrument, Mol-Instructions. The linked instrument is described as a dataset covering molecule generation tasks, where retrosynthesis predicts the possible precursors that lead to a given target molecule.
