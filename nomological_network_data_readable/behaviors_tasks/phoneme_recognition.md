# Phoneme recognition
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** Phone recognition  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** The available papers converge on a speech task at the phonetic-unit level, but differ slightly in emphasis. One defines it as recognizing and distinguishing individual phonemes within speech audio, while another defines it as transcribing speech into a sequence of constituent phonetic units. What counts as the behavior is therefore framed either as identifying individual phonemes or as producing a phonetic-unit sequence from speech.

**Variants.** Papers use both the names phoneme recognition and phone recognition. The described setting is speech audio, and one paper abbreviates the task as PR and places it among multiple instruction-tuning tasks. No other sub-tasks, domains, or modalities are named.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to it.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** Papers do not link it to any construct.
