**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** The only explicit framing treats essay scoring as the automatic evaluation of a language learner's writing skills from a text they have produced. In UniversalCEFR: Enabling Open Multilingual Research on Language Proficiency Assessment, the task is described as evaluating "the writing skills of the learners as reflected in a text they have written," and the paper also names it automated essay scoring (AES).

**Variants.** The available paper uses automated essay scoring (AES) as the label for the task. It does not describe separate sub-tasks, domains, modalities, or alternative success criteria.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to essay scoring by papers.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to essay scoring by papers.
