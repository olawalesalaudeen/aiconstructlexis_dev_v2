# Personality mirroring
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 2 papers  
**Also known as:** Conversational adaptation, Linguistic coordination, Tone imitation  

## State of the Field

The observable alignment of language use between conversational partners at the level of the entire dialogue.

## Sources

> The observable act of a chatbot adopting the linguistic style, tone, and behavioral tendencies of the user it is interacting with.
