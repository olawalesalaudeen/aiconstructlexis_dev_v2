# Chart summarization
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the cited work, chart summarization is most commonly treated as producing a textual summary or analysis of chart content, rather than directly answering a single factual question. The prevailing formulation is open-ended: one paper describes it as generating a concise summary of the trends, insights, or information in a chart, and another explicitly places it among tasks with "questions lacking a single definitive answer."

**Variants.** Papers place the behavior in slightly different task frames rather than giving many named subtypes. One source situates it within broader chart reasoning, described as "chart analysis, summarization, and etc.," and also discusses it alongside analysis and editing of charts.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to this behavior.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to it.
