# Dependency parsing
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 4 papers  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the papers, dependency parsing is consistently treated as a task of sentence-level syntactic analysis: assigning directed dependency relations among sentence elements. The prevailing wording describes analyzing the grammatical structure of a sentence and establishing dependencies between words, while a closely matching formulation describes assigning syntactic dependency relations between tokens in a sentence.

**Variants.** Papers do not name sub-tasks, domains, or modalities for this behavior. The main variation in framing is that one paper treats it as a linguistically motivated token-classification task over tokens, while another describes it more generally in terms of sentence structure and dependencies between words.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to dependency parsing. In the papers, it is described directly as a task, including one framing of it as a token-classification task.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to dependency parsing. The papers define the behavior, but do not explicitly connect it to a broader construct that it indicates.
