# Image quality assessment
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** Image quality analysis  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** The available definition treats image quality assessment as a vision-language model's ability to understand, recognize, and assess image quality, including identifying specific types of degradation. In "An Intelligent Agentic System for Complex Image Restoration Problems," this behavior is also framed as the ability to analyze the quality of images generated at each stage of a processing pipeline.

**Variants.** The paper uses closely related formulations that expand the behavior into recognizing complex image degradations, quality assessment, quality comparison, and reasoning about image quality. It also situates the task in a staged image-processing setting, where images are assessed throughout the pipeline.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to this behavior in the papers represented here.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to this behavior in the papers represented here, so the available work does not explicitly present it as evidence of another construct.
