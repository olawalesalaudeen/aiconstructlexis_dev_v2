# Mistake remediation
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 3 papers  
**Also known as:** Mistake correction, Student mistake remediation  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the surveyed work, the prevailing usage treats mistake remediation as a response task in which the model gives feedback, guidance, or another response that helps a student fix an error. Definitions largely agree on that goal, though some wording focuses on an identified error in the student's work, while another framing centers on a student's utterance that contains a mistake or confusion and counts success as helping the student correct it.

**Variants.** Papers use closely related names for this behavior, especially "Mistake correction" and "Student mistake remediation." The narrower wording centers on correcting an identified error, while the broader wording also includes confusion in a student's utterance and says the tutor responds in order to remediate such mistakes or confusions. One paper also describes students' mistake and confusion remediation as "one of the most popular" tasks, and another discusses mistake correction alongside mistake location.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to this behavior, so papers do not identify a recurring instrument for eliciting or scoring it.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** Papers do not link this behavior to any construct, so the available work does not specify what broader construct it is taken to indicate.
