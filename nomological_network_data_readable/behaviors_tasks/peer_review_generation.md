# Peer review generation
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  
**Also known as:** Review writing, Paper review generation  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the surveyed papers, this behavior is treated as generating a review-style evaluation of an academic paper. One definition frames it as producing critiques and numerical scores from the paper's content and its context in a community graph, while another frames it as structured feedback from content analysis that covers strengths, weaknesses, and acceptance decisions. The snippets further describe the output as "delivering the final score, decision and comments" and as "generating high-quality, insightful evaluations."

**Variants.** Papers use the near-synonymous labels "review writing" and "paper review generation," with some variation in setting and conditioning. One paper studies it as "the final stage of the automatic research simulation" and labels it "Stage 3: Review Writing," whereas the other centers on the final review package itself. The available definitions also differ in what information the review is based on, either the paper's content plus community-graph context or content analysis alone. One paper also reports it as related to Text generation, placing it after paper reading and paper writing, with each stage using the previous stage's output as input.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to it. In practice, papers operationalize the behavior through the review artifacts themselves, such as critiques with numerical scores, or strengths, weaknesses, decisions, and comments.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to it as evidence, so the papers do not record a construct that this behavior is taken to indicate here. Beyond construct links, the only recorded connection is a related link to Text generation in one paper.
