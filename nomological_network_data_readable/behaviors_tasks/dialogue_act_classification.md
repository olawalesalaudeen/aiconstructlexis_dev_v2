# Dialogue act classification
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 5 papers  
**Also known as:** Dialogue act recognition  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the definitions, the prevailing usage treats it as the task of labeling or classifying an utterance in a dialogue by its communicative function or intent. Most descriptions stay at the utterance or speaker-turn level within a dialogue, and they largely agree on the task formulation, differing mainly between "classification" and "recognition" wording and between "function" and "intent" language.

**Variants.** Papers use both "dialogue act classification" and "dialogue act recognition," and one study abbreviates the latter as DAR. Some papers place it in empathy-oriented dialogue settings, referring to dialogue roles and acts in EmpDial Role and EmpDial QAct and to labels of empathic intents directly at the dialogue act level in EmpDial QInt and EI. Another recognition setting describes the target speaker turn as aggregating information from neighboring turns according to a graph structure.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to it, so the papers do not identify a recurring instrument that elicits or scores this behavior.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to it, so the papers do not specify a construct that this behavior is taken to reflect.
