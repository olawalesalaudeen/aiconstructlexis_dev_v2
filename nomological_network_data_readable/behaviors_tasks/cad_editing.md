# CAD editing
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 2 papers  
**Also known as:** Masked CAD sequence generation, CAD sequence infilling, Iterative editing  

## State of the Field

Identifying regions in a CAD sequence that require modification by replacing them with <mask> tokens while preserving unmodified parts.
