# Contradiction detection
**Type:** Behavior  
**Referenced in:** 14 papers  
**Also known as:** Document-level self-contradiction detection, Contradiction identification, Conflict detection, Answer conflict detection, Corpus-level inconsistency detection, Contradiction checking  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across papers, the prevailing usage is a judgment about whether pieces of content are logically inconsistent in context. Most definitions ask for detecting the presence of a contradiction, often as a binary decision, whether the items are a multi-sentence document, two generated sentences, a new fact against an existing world state, or retrieved external knowledge against internal knowledge. A smaller line of work makes the behavior more selective rather than purely binary, requiring the model to choose the testimony-evidence pair that contradicts, in a task described as "essentially a multiple-choice format with an action space of |T| × |E|". Papers largely agree on the core criterion of logical incompatibility, and some state it formally as a conflict where a state entails both φ and ¬φ.

**Variants.** Papers use both contradiction detection and conflict detection, spanning document, corpus, multilingual answer, retrieval-augmented, and narrative or world-state settings. The narrative line includes checking whether overlapping validity intervals contradict existing facts and detecting "conflict quadruples" based on time order. Other isolated formulations place the same behavior in multi-agent reasoning, SAT solving, or legal clause-provision comparison.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to it. Even so, papers most commonly operationalize the behavior as a binary judgment, for example binary classification over atomic facts, deciding whether two sentences are contradictory within context, or checking whether a clause conforms to or contradicts a provision. A smaller set uses structured selection rather than yes or no scoring, including a multiple-choice search over testimony-evidence pairs.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** Papers do not present a shared construct that this behavior measures. The only linked construct is Knowledge conflict resolution, and one paper treats contradiction detection as related to it, describing a framework that can "identify and resolve detailed points of knowledge conflict." Beyond that related link, the papers do not converge on a broader evidence-of role.
