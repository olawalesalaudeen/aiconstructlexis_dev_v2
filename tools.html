Analysis support

Tools for planning validity analyses from the Lexis. Pick a construct or a benchmark and get every instrument, contrast, or claim the literature offers, with the papers and evidence behind each one.

The Lexis records what papers claim: that a paper used an instrument to measure a construct, or stated that two things are related. These tools plan analyses from those claims. A row here means "N papers link these", not that the link is valid. Click Evidence on any row to read what the papers say.

Every tool lets you filter by venue and by how many papers support a link, open the verbatim evidence behind any row, export results as CSV or JSON, export the supporting papers as a list of titles, venues, and links, and copy a link that reopens the exact view.
